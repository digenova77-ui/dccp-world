The only honest target set: public registries, feeds, and commons the venue *elects* to ingest. Publish-to-the-commons, never write-to-their-database. **DCLM (ruling — law, physics, philosophy):**

- **Physics:** The mechanism is ingestion-by-compliant-construction. Atom-level manifestation is struck as poetry. What remains is describable: publish complete artifacts → venue pipelines ingest → venue logging records.
