**Trinity bears down hardest here (by design):**

## §4 — Emission mechanics: merit-gated, peg-calibrated

The design problem asked: emission via energy? via merit? **Both — in distinct roles.** Merit gates (WHO the emission goes to — the measure); the energy peg denominates (HOW MUCH — the calibration). Neither alone suffices: merit without a peg is unanchored; a peg without merit is unearned.

The per-epoch cycle:

1. **Work → receipts.** All work in all three zones produces receipts; Iris gates label every figure REPORTED / MODELED / UNKNOWN. UNKNOWN never pays, never scores.
2. **Receipts → merit.** Gated receipts accrue merit to the worker's Unity ID (§6). No receipt, no merit — by construction, not by policy.
3. **Merit → emission (computed).** At epoch close, each pool computes its epoch emission: merit-weighted across its members' verified merit, calibrated to the energy peg, bounded by remaining pool authority (50M lifetime cap per pool).
4. **Peg calibration.** The merit→eFuse conversion rate is set so emitted eFuse tracks the energy peg: **1 eFuse ≡ E real-world energy units (E TBD — David's digit, §15).** If the epoch's merit implies emission that would break the peg, the excess is held in the Peg Regulation Reserve (§5.5) — members are never paid beyond the peg. The peg is the definition of the unit; the calibration keeps the definition honest.
5. **Disbursement.** eFuse moves to the earning Unity IDs — every movement Unity-bound, every movement receipted (§8, §11).
6. **Review.** Epoch review (human constitutional mandate + timelock) sets next epoch's parameters *from the receipt record* — bands, thresholds, calibration. Parameters before evidence are MODELED at best; the receipts take it from there. Economic parameter changes need both halves (bicameral); deadlock = no change, and operational emission continues under the last-set parameters (pre-authorized mandate — the economy does not halt on governance stall).

**Cut (named):** *pre-funding refused* (ratified Q4, restated because the temptation never dies): no pool is ever funded before its receipts exist. Paying before the receipt is the original sin of every broken tokenomics; the gates forbid it here.

## §6 — Merit's exact function

**Working definition** (David's precise definition still his word — marked his-to-confirm): merit is the protocol-fidelity measure — "merit wins the work." The mechanics below are **definition-agnostic by construction**: whatever precise meaning his word gives "protocol-fidelity," merit remains a non-transferable, Unity-bound, receipt-anchored score that gates emission and work access. The interface survives the definition.

**Accrual:** merit accrues only from gated receipts (F8) — work merit, wave merit, social merit, corroboration merit, execution/verification merit, bridge merit (§3). Each receipt carries a merit weight from the receipt-anchored bands (Q6); the weight is posted plainly on the receipt (Iris: the reason is visible).

**Binding:** merit is bound to the Unity ID that earned it. Non-transferable, non-tradable, non-purchasable — it is a measure of the member, not a currency. It cannot be sent, sold, gifted, or inherited. (Inheritance is a human question for David's word, not this doc's.)

**Gating — what merit does:**
1. **Gates emission:** eFuse emits to an ID in proportion to its verified merit (§4). No merit, no emission.
2. **Wins the work:** higher merit → access to higher bounty bands → more and better work → more emission. Merit compounds through *work*, never through itself.
3. **Gates bridging rights:** only IDs above the bridge-merit threshold may post or fulfill cross-pool bounties (Zone M) — the mesh is premium work and premium-watched; the threshold is a parameter (TBD).

**Amplification — tiers and rings:** merit weights scale with social depth — the tier factor (family > good friend > friend; verified kin highest) multiplies social merit events, and the ring-depth factor multiplies work done within deeper verified rings. The structure rewards structure: the deeper the verified social graph around the work, the more the work is worth. Both factors are parameters (TBD, §15); both require *verified* depth (L2 umpires — self-claimed depth earns zero, §3.1 cut).

**Decay:** merit decays per epoch — fidelity is current, not historical. Ancient glory does not emit. The decay rate is a parameter (TBD, §15); the principle is law: *merit measures who you are being, not who you were.*

**Cut (named):** *merit interest refused.* Merit never earns merit on its own — no staking, no yield, no "merit begets merit." Merit grows only through new gated receipts. Anything else would let standing compound into weight, and standing never weight is Genesis law.

## §7 — Honor: the donation class (and why donations don't earn Merit)

Donations — eFuse to the Lock (F4), fiat to the gateway (F5) — accrue **Honor**, not Merit. Honor is public, named, permanent, Unity-bound, non-transferable — and it **never converts to emission**.

This is the brightest line in the tokenomics, drawn deliberately:

- If donations earned Merit, and Merit gates emission, then **fiat could buy eFuse through a side door** — donate fiat → merit → emission. That would make "nobody ever buys or sells that coin" a slogan with an exception, and exceptions are where illogical circles re-enter (the bucket has a name for this one).
- So the design refuses it outright: **there is no path from fiat to eFuse — not direct, not indirect, not clever.** The monetary law holds absolutely.
- The reward for giving is the giver's currency: **recognition, legacy, and Eden itself.** Honor is posted plainly (Iris — the gift and the giver named, forever), it never decays (legacy is permanent — the deliberate asymmetry with Merit's decay), and the donation receipt is a permanent record. The entire charitable world runs on this reward; it is real, it is human (Twain²), and it cannot be farmed into money.

**Cut (named):** *donation→merit→eFuse refused.* This was the obvious gameable design and it is out — stated here so nobody re-proposes it quietly later. The gift is the gift.

## §10 — Peg regulation: the only internal movement

**The peg:** 1 eFuse ≡ E real-world energy units. **E is David's digit** (HELD, §15) — it denominates the entire economy and nothing here invents it.

**The regulation:** at each epoch close, the computed emission is checked against the peg. **What the peg does without a market:** it is the unit of account — it denominates the merit→eFuse conversion (§4.4), it measures the cause-work (waves are physical: energy, water, food — the peg gives the Lock's disbursements a real-world measure), and it is the promise the Reserve keeps. "Pegged to energy" means the coin *means* energy, even where no one trades it.

**Cut (named):** *peg defense by market operations refused* — there is no market to operate in, by law. The peg is held by calibration and the Reserve, never by buying or selling. The law forbids the tool; the design doesn't miss it.

## §11 — Receipt anchoring and idempotency
