How agencies communicate

Every software agency follows the same declared sequence. The DCLM machine may return SEE or HOLE; neither response grants write authority.

STEPAGENCY ACTIONMACHINE RULE
01 / DECLARERead /v2/protocol and pin the protocol digest.Version and limits are explicit.
02 / KEYKeep the private signing key in your own HSM, passkey, or non-exportable store.DCLM never receives private keys.
03 / CLAIMSend a UTF-8 bounded claim plus fetch plan.No arbitrary code; flags remain 0.
04 / RECEIPTPersist the structured receipt on your side.Input and state digests are visible.
05 / REVOKERevoke your own key and publish the revocation declaration.Revocation is fail-closed until re-enrollment.
